Install kaise karein — 3 asaan step

  1. Upar wala Download button dabao — file download ho jayegi.
  2. Browser ya phone puche to "Install anyway" / "Allow from this source" karo — ye Play Store ke bahar ki file hai, isliye phone pehle confirm karta hai.
  3. App kholo aur Google se login karo — bas, shuru ho jao.

App me kya-kya hai

  • Roz ka check-in — chat se ya form se, 2 minute me apni tabiyat ka record.
  • Record + chart — purani entries aur graph me dekho, tabiyat behtar ho rahi hai ya nahi.
  • Doctors ki list — apne doctors ke naam-number ek jagah, tap karo aur call lag jaye.
  • Sinus guide — naak band, sar dard aur sinus ki samajh, asaan Hinglish me.
  • AI se sawal — dawai aur health ke sawal poochho; dawai ki photo bhejo, app uski pehchan karke jaankari deta hai.
