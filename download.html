How to install — 3 easy steps

  1. Tap the Download button above — the file will download.
  2. If the browser or phone asks, choose "Install anyway" / "Allow from this source" — this file is outside the Play Store, so the phone confirms first.
  3. Open the app and log in with Google — that is it, you are ready to start.

What is inside the app

  • Daily check-in — by chat or form, record how you feel in 2 minutes.
  • Record + chart — see old entries and the graph to know whether your health is improving.
  • Doctors list — your doctors' names and numbers in one place; tap and the call connects.
  • Health guide — blocked nose, headache, sinus and much more, explained in simple language.
  • Ask the AI — ask medicine and health questions; send a medicine photo and the app identifies it and gives you the info.
🔒 Privacy: Your data is visible only to you — login-protected. No data is ever sold.
